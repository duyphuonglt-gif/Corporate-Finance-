**1. Executive Summary**

iQIYI Inc.\'s FY2025 financial results reveal a company whose operating
fundamentals have materially deteriorated relative to FY2024. The most
urgent signal is a Times Interest Earned (TIE) ratio of 0.25x: INC_ebit
of RMB 229.3M covers only 25% of INC_interest_expense of RMB 909.6M,
producing a net loss of RMB 204.0M and a net profit margin of --0.75%,
reversing FY2024\'s positive net margin of +2.7%. The debt burden factor
of --0.427x in the Du Pont decomposition is the proximate cause of
negative Return on Equity (ROE) of --1.53%, even as Return on Assets
(ROA) remains marginally positive at 1.04%.

At the market level, iQIYI trades at a significant discount to book
value: Market Value Added (MVA) of --RMB 5,362.7M and a Market-to-Book
ratio of 0.597x signal that the capital markets assign substantially
less value to the firm than its reported equity base --- a reflection of
the accumulated retained-earnings deficit of --RMB 42.7B. Economic Value
Added (EVA) of --RMB 1,570.3M confirms that operating returns fall well
short of the firm\'s cost of capital. Gross margin contracted 380 basis
points year-over-year (24.9% FY2024 to 21.1% FY2025), while liquidity
remains critically strained: current ratio of 0.47x and net working
capital (NWC) of --RMB 11.8B. Immediate management attention is required
on debt service capacity, content cost discipline, and working capital
structure.

**2. Ratio Analysis by Category**

**2.1 Performance Ratios**

iQIYI\'s performance ratios collectively indicate that the firm is
destroying economic value at both the accounting and market levels. MVA
stands at --RMB 5,362.7M, computed as market_capitalization_rmb of RMB
7,946.2M minus currentYear_equity of RMB 13,308.9M. This deeply negative
figure reflects not merely a valuation discount but a structural capital
destruction signal: the market assigns a value to the firm\'s equity
that is RMB 5.4B below its balance-sheet carrying value. The primary
accounting explanation is the accumulated retained-earnings deficit of
--RMB 42.7B (BAL_retained_earnings_curr), which overwhelms paid-in
capital of RMB 56.0B. The net result is positive book equity of only RMB
13.3B, a thin cushion relative to total liabilities of RMB 33.4B.
Market-to-Book of 0.597x (below the 1.0x threshold) indicates that
investors price the equity at a 40.3% discount to reported net asset
value, consistent with chronic unprofitability and high execution risk
in the PRC streaming sector.

EVA of --RMB 1,570.3M quantifies the gap between what the business
earned on an after-tax operating basis and the minimum required by
capital providers. currentYear_after_tax_operating_income of RMB 478.2M
was insufficient to cover the capital charge of RMB 2,048.5M
(cost_capital of 9% applied to startYear_total_capitalization of RMB
22,761.2M). Approximately 77% of the EVA shortfall is attributable to
the high capital charge rather than zero operating income --- iQIYI does
generate positive after-tax operating returns, but its WACC hurdle is
demanding given the embedded 3% China risk premium. This finding implies
that even a modest improvement in operating income would not close the
EVA gap without simultaneous deleveraging or WACC reduction.

**2.2 Profitability Ratios**

The divergence between ROA (1.04%) and ROE (--1.53%) is the central
profitability story. ROA uses currentYear_after_tax_operating_income
(RMB 478.2M) as the numerator, a figure that adds back the after-tax
cost of debt to net income. This measure is positive, confirming that
iQIYI\'s assets generate modest operating returns before the cost of
debt is considered. ROE, by contrast, uses INC_net (--RMB 204.0M), which
fully absorbs INC_interest_expense of RMB 909.6M. The delta between the
two ratios (255 basis points) is the leverage/interest burden story:
financial leverage of 3.508x amplifies the interest cost to the point of
reversing the sign of equity returns.

Operating profit margin of 1.75% (INC_ebit of RMB 229.3M divided by
INC_sales of RMB 27,291.3M) is insufficient to service the firm\'s debt
load. The bridge from operating profit margin to net profit margin
(--0.75%) runs primarily through INC_interest_expense: RMB 909.6M of
interest against INC_ebit of only RMB 229.3M produces a pre-tax loss
before other income adjustments. Other income (INC_other_income = RMB
620.8M) partially offsets, but the residual taxable income is --RMB
59.5M, and additional tax expense of RMB 144.5M drives the net loss to
RMB 204.0M. Gross margin compression from 24.9% (FY2024) to 21.1%
(FY2025) --- a 380-basis-point deterioration --- is the upstream driver,
reflecting elevated content cost embedded in INC_cost_goods_sold of RMB
21,542.3M. With a gross margin of only 21.1%, SG&A of RMB 5,519.6M
consumes virtually all of gross profit, leaving operating margin at a
structurally vulnerable 1.75%.

**2.3 Efficiency Ratios**

Asset turnover of 0.596x (INC_sales of RMB 27,291.3M divided by
startYear_total_assets of RMB 45,760.5M) reflects the capital-intensive
nature of iQIYI\'s content library. The streaming business requires
sustained, upfront investment in licensed and original programming ---
assets that are predominantly long-duration and illiquid. As revenue
contracts relative to the asset base, asset turnover declines,
compressing the Du Pont ROA channel. The fixed-cost nature of the
content library means that revenue shortfalls do not produce
proportional asset reductions; instead, the turnover ratio deteriorates
mechanically.

Inventory turnover of 55.4x and Days in Inventory of 6.6 days appear
anomalously high for a content company, but are economically rational:
BAL_inventories represents licensed copyrights carried at amortized
cost. These assets are rapidly amortized through INC_cost_goods_sold as
content is delivered to subscribers, producing a high turnover velocity
relative to the carrying balance. The metric does not indicate supply
chain efficiency in the traditional sense; rather, it reflects iQIYI\'s
content recognition accounting policy.

Average Collection Period of 29.3 days (startYear_receivables of RMB
2,191.2M divided by currentYear_daily_sales_average of RMB 74.8M) is
operationally reasonable for a platform business collecting subscription
and advertising revenues. However, in the context of the macro
advertising downturn affecting PRC digital media in FY2025, receivables
quality warrants scrutiny. Advertising revenue is more exposed to
counterparty default risk than subscription revenue, and a 29.3-day
collection cycle does not provide a margin of safety if advertiser
credit conditions deteriorate further.

**2.4 Leverage Ratios**

TIE of 0.25x is the single most critical red flag in iQIYI\'s FY2025
financials. INC_ebit of RMB 229.3M covers only one-quarter of
INC_interest_expense of RMB 909.6M. This ratio directly explains why net
income is negative despite positive operating income: operating earnings
are insufficient to service the debt stack. From a solvency perspective,
a TIE below 1.0x implies that the firm cannot service its interest
obligations from operating income alone, creating dependency on
non-operating income (RMB 620.8M) and asset liquidation to bridge the
gap. If non-operating income declines or becomes unavailable, covenant
violations and liquidity pressure would follow rapidly.

Cash Coverage of 14.83x provides an apparent contradiction to TIE. The
numerator adds CASH_depreciation_amortization of RMB 13,264.1M --- the
D&A add-back from the operating section of the Cash Flow Statement ---
to INC_ebit, producing a coverage ratio of nearly 15x. The divergence is
not a paradox but a reflection of iQIYI\'s accounting structure: D&A is
embedded in INC_cost_goods_sold rather than disclosed separately on the
income statement (INC_depreciation = 0). This means the firm\'s EBIT is
substantially depressed relative to its cash generation capacity. In
cash flow terms, iQIYI generates meaningful operating cash
(CASH_operating = RMB 105.8M), and the D&A add-back reveals that most of
the cost structure is non-cash content amortization. The lesson is that
TIE understates cash-based debt service capacity, while Cash Coverage
overstates it to the extent that D&A represents real economic cost of
content replenishment.

Total Debt Ratio of 71.5% (currentYear_liabilities_total of RMB
33,372.8M divided by currentYear_assets_total of RMB 46,681.7M) reflects
iQIYI\'s convertible note structure: PAG Notes of USD 550M at 6% and
2028 Notes of USD 208M at 6.5% are USD-denominated obligations serviced
from primarily RMB operating cash flows. Long-Term Debt Ratio of 43.1%
and Long-Term Debt-Equity of 0.757x capture the structural leverage,
while the overall debt ratio underscores that creditors, not equity
holders, bear the majority of the firm\'s asset financing risk.

**2.5 Liquidity Ratios**

Current Ratio of 0.47x and NWC of --RMB 11.8B are severe on their face,
but must be interpreted within the economics of a subscription streaming
platform. A significant portion of currentYear_liabilities_current (RMB
22,067.3M) consists of deferred subscription revenue --- cash already
collected from subscribers for services not yet delivered. This
liability is discharged by providing content rather than by cash
outflow, which mechanically depresses the current ratio without
necessarily signaling imminent default. Content payables cycle
similarly: accounts payable of RMB 6,652.4M represents obligations to
content suppliers that are partially offset by the amortization of
already-recognized content assets.

That said, the liquidity position is not without genuine risk. Cash
Ratio of 0.20x (currentYear_cash_marketable_securities of RMB 4,354.3M
divided by currentYear_liabilities_current of RMB 22,067.3M) implies
that liquid assets cover only 20% of current obligations.
BAL_debt_short_term_curr of RMB 4,690.6M --- current-year debt
maturities --- is essentially matched by the total cash balance, leaving
no buffer for operational disruptions. Quick Ratio of 0.31x (adding
receivables to cash, divided by current liabilities) confirms that even
near-liquid assets fall substantially short of current liabilities. The
firm\'s ability to meet near-term obligations rests on the continuity of
subscriber cash inflows and the rollover of maturing debt tranches.

**3. Du Pont Decomposition**

The four-factor Du Pont model decomposes ROE into its operational and
financial components, isolating the precise source of value destruction:

Factor 1 --- Debt Burden (--0.427x): This is the single factor
responsible for ROE being negative. RATIO_debt_burden equals INC_net
divided by currentYear_after_tax_operating_income: --RMB 204.0M divided
by RMB 478.2M = --0.427x. The negative sign is the mathematical
mechanism by which ROE flips from positive to negative. Even with
positive Leverage, Asset Turnover, and Operating Profit Margin,
multiplying by --0.427x reverses the product\'s sign. The business
generates positive after-tax operating returns (RMB 478.2M), but
interest expense of RMB 909.6M exceeds EBIT of RMB 229.3M, making the
net income negative.

Factor 2 --- Leverage (3.508x): RATIO_leverage equals
currentYear_assets_total divided by currentYear_equity (RMB 46,681.7M /
RMB 13,308.9M). Under normal conditions --- when Debt Burden is positive
--- leverage amplifies ROE. In iQIYI\'s case, leverage amplifies the
loss: a higher asset-to-equity ratio makes the negative Debt Burden more
destructive to equity returns. This is the reverse-leverage effect
inherent to overleveraged firms with sub-1.0x interest coverage.

Factor 3 --- Asset Turnover (0.596x): RATIO_asset_turnover of 0.596x
reflects iQIYI\'s capital-intensive content model. Revenue of RMB 27.3B
supported by assets of RMB 45.8B (prior year base) produces a
below-average turnover for a technology/media platform. Increasing this
factor requires either growing revenue without proportional asset growth
or selectively rationalizing the asset base --- both challenging given
the fixed-cost content library.

Factor 4 --- Operating Profit Margin (1.75%):
RATIO_operating_profit_margin of 1.75% leaves no buffer for adverse
operating shocks. Any further gross margin compression or SG&A elevation
would push operating income to zero, eliminating even the modest
positive contribution from this factor.

Methodological Note --- Du Pont vs. Direct ROE: Du Pont ROE (--1.56%)
differs from direct ROE (--1.53%) by 3 basis points. RATIO_leverage uses
currentYear_equity (FY2025 year-end) as its denominator, while direct
ROE uses startYear_equity (FY2024 year-end). Both formulations are
internally consistent; the difference is a methodological artifact, not
a computation error.

Conclusion: To restore positive ROE, iQIYI must first resolve the Debt
Burden factor --- either by growing INC_ebit past the interest expense
threshold or by reducing INC_interest_expense through debt
repurchase/refinancing. Once Debt Burden turns positive, the existing
Leverage ratio (3.508x) will amplify positive returns rather than
losses. Asset Turnover improvement is a secondary priority, conditional
on resolving the debt burden first.

**4. Strategic Recommendations**

1.  **Prioritize Debt Restructuring to Address Critical Interest
    Coverage (TIE = 0.25x)**

TIE of 0.25x is the most urgent financial risk: INC_ebit of RMB 229.3M
covers only 25% of INC_interest_expense of RMB 909.6M, and
RATIO_debt_burden of --0.427x is the primary driver of negative ROE.
Required direction: reduce INC_interest_expense relative to INC_ebit,
either by growing operating income or by repurchasing high-coupon notes.
Operationally, management should evaluate accelerating repurchase of PAG
Notes (USD 550M at 6%) ahead of the 2028 maturity, deploying available
cash (RMB 4.35B) toward buyback rather than reinvestment if content ROI
is marginal. Alternatively, refinancing USD-denominated notes into
lower-rate RMB instruments would reduce both interest expense and
currency mismatch. Constraint: executing debt repurchase while
maintaining Cash Ratio of 0.20x against current liabilities of RMB 22.1B
creates liquidity tension. Management must model the post-repurchase
cash ratio and ensure minimum liquidity covenants are preserved.

2.  **Implement Content Cost Discipline to Recover Gross Margin (21.1%
    vs. 24.9% Prior Year)**

Gross margin compression from 24.9% to 21.1% (380 bps) is the upstream
driver of thin operating margin (1.75%). INC_cost_goods_sold of RMB
21,542.3M on INC_sales of RMB 27,291.3M leaves only RMB 5,748.9M of
gross profit, barely covering INC_sga of RMB 5,519.6M. Required
direction: reduce content cost per unit of revenue by (a) shifting the
content mix toward lower-cost original productions and licensed
catalogue content, (b) renegotiating or allowing high-cost exclusive
licenses to expire, and (c) implementing return-on-content analytics to
exit content investments below hurdle rate. Each 100 bps of gross margin
recovery translates to approximately RMB 273M of additional gross
profit, which at scale could move INC_ebit from RMB 229.3M toward the
interest coverage threshold. Constraint: content quality deterioration
risks subscriber churn, which would reduce INC_sales and worsen all
revenue-dependent ratios further. The trade-off between cost reduction
and content investment must be managed carefully.

3.  **Improve Asset Productivity to Address Low Asset Turnover
    (0.596x)**

Asset turnover of 0.596x reflects an asset base of RMB 46.7B generating
only RMB 27.3B in revenue. RATIO_asset_turnover is the Du Pont channel
through which asset productivity feeds into ROE. Required direction:
increase revenue per unit of invested assets by (a) growing subscription
ARPU (average revenue per user) through premium tier pricing, (b)
monetizing the content library through B2B licensing and distribution
deals, and (c) selectively disposing of or not renewing underperforming
content assets to reduce BAL_other_assets without proportional revenue
loss. Asset turnover improvement of 0.1x (to 0.696x) on a stable asset
base would add approximately RMB 4.6B in revenue, materially improving
operating leverage across fixed content costs. Constraint: iQIYI\'s
content library is largely fixed in the near term; asset disposal
requires identifying assets with near-zero revenue contribution and
navigating PRC content licensing regulations that may restrict secondary
market transactions.

4.  **Restructure Working Capital to Address Current Ratio of 0.47x and
    NWC of --RMB 11.8B**

Current Ratio of 0.47x and NWC of --RMB 11.8B indicate that current
liabilities (RMB 22.1B) are more than double current assets (RMB 10.3B).
While deferred subscription revenue partially explains the structural
negative NWC, BAL_debt_short_term_curr of RMB 4.7B represents near-term
debt maturities that require cash or refinancing. Required direction:
extend the maturity profile of short-term debt by proactively
refinancing short-duration tranches into longer-tenor instruments before
they roll into current liabilities. Additionally, accelerating
subscriber cash collection through annual prepaid plans improves cash
inflows and reduces accounts receivable days. Management should target a
current ratio above 0.60x as a minimum operating buffer. Constraint:
refinancing short-term debt in a rising-rate environment may increase
blended interest cost, potentially worsening TIE. Tenor extension must
be evaluated against the interest cost trade-off.

5.  **Mitigate VIE/Currency/Regulatory Risk --- USD Debt Against RMB
    Cash Flows**

iQIYI\'s PAG Notes (USD 550M at 6%) and 2028 Notes (USD 208M at 6.5%)
create USD-denominated interest and principal obligations against
predominantly RMB operating cash flows. At fx_rate of 6.9931, total USD
note exposure approximates RMB 5.3B in principal alone. Any RMB
depreciation relative to USD directly inflates the functional-currency
cost of debt service, worsening TIE and cash coverage beyond model
projections. Additionally, iQIYI\'s VIE structure introduces PRC
regulatory exposure --- content bans, data-security reviews, and HNTE
license renewal risk --- that the current WACC of 9% (including a 3%
China risk premium) may not fully capture. Required actions: management
should implement cross-currency interest rate swaps on a portion of USD
note exposure, denominating a portion of interest payments in RMB. WACC
reassessment triggers should be established formally: any of the
following would require immediate re-evaluation --- (a) RMB/USD movement
beyond 5% in either direction, (b) HNTE license non-renewal, or (c) PRC
content regulatory action affecting more than 10% of the active content
library. Constraint: hedging instruments carry their own cost and basis
risk, and PRC capital account restrictions may limit the effectiveness
of certain derivative structures.
